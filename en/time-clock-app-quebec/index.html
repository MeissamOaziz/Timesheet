A punch clock app made in Québec, for Québec businesses

PunchClock Crew is a time-and-attendance app built in Québec. The whole app, every report and every automatic email work in French or English, and prices are in Canadian dollars.

What you get

  • Employees clock in and out with a 4-digit PIN on a shared tablet, computer or phone at your location. No app to install.
  • Several locations under one account, each with its own employees and clock-in terminal.
  • Hours, overtime and evening hours calculated for you, then exported to PDF, Excel or CSV for payroll.
  • Québec statutory holidays added to the schedule in one click (Scheduling add-on).
  • Time-off requests, an employee portal and automatic pay-period emails, including a payroll summary for your accountant.

Who uses it

Shops, restaurants, clinics, garages, farms, cleaning companies and other teams that work in shifts across Québec and the rest of Canada.

Questions

Is PunchClock Crew available in French?

Yes. The interface, the reports and the automatic emails are fully available in French and in English, and you can switch language at any time.

Where is my data stored and how is it protected?

Data is stored with Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security, passwords are hashed and every connection uses HTTPS. The app was designed with Québec’s Law 25 on personal information in mind: it asks employees only for what it needs, and you can ask for your data to be deleted.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The Free plan is free with no time limit and no credit card, and every new account previews the paid features for 14 days.
