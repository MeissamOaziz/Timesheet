Punch, schedules and time off in one Canadian app

Time clock with GPS and geofencing

Geofencing draws an invisible boundary around each of your sites. A punch from inside the boundary goes through; a punch from home or from the car is blocked, and the employee sees how far away they are.

Start freeTry the live demo

How it works

  • You set a radius per site, from 10 m for a small store to 500 m for a large facility.
  • Lenient mode (the default) lets a punch through when GPS is unavailable and shows a warning. Strict mode blocks it.
  • Each punch records the GPS position and the distance from the site, shown as a map pin in the activity log.

For crews on the road

  • Turn on remote clock-in and grant it to the employees who need it. They punch in from their own phone through the employee portal, and you see where each punch was made.
  • A fixed tablet at the entrance is still the simplest option for teams who all work at one place.

Questions

Which plan includes geofencing?

Geofencing and remote clock-in are included from the Starter plan ($19.49/month CAD). New accounts can try them for 14 days.

What if an employee turns off location?

In lenient mode the punch goes through with a warning. In strict mode it is blocked. You choose per site.

Do employees need to install an app?

No. Employees use the web on a shared device or, for remote clock-in, their own phone through the employee portal.
