Punch, schedules and time off in one Canadian app

An online punch clock on a tablet you already own

An online punch clock does the job of the time clock machine and its punch cards, with no machine to buy and no cards to add up. With PunchClock Crew, the punch clock is a web page open on your own tablet, fixed at the entrance.

What you need

  • A tablet, computer or phone you already have, with a web browser and an internet connection: an iPad, an Android tablet, a Windows PC or a Mac.
  • A PunchClock Crew account. The Free plan is enough for 1 site and up to 10 active employees.
  • That is all. PunchClock Crew is software: we do not sell, rent or supply any device.

How your employees punch

  • The employee picks their name, punches in or out and confirms with their 4-digit PIN.
  • Each employee has their own PIN, and the page locks for a while after several wrong PINs.
  • The dashboard shows right away who is at work.

What replaces the punch cards

  • Hours are added up for you: no more cards to total by hand at the end of the period.
  • PDF reports for any date range, and Excel or CSV export for payroll from the Starter plan.
  • A manager fixes a forgotten punch, and the correction stays in the audit log (Starter plan and up).
  • Several locations: each site has its own clock-in page, and you see everything in the same account.

Questions

Do I need to buy a punch clock machine?

No. PunchClock Crew is software. You use a tablet, computer or phone you already have, and we do not sell any device.

Does it work on a computer?

Yes. The clock-in page runs in a web browser on a Windows PC, a Mac, an iPad or an Android tablet.

What happens if the internet goes down?

The clock-in page needs an internet connection to record a punch. If the connection drops, a manager can add the missing punches afterwards (Starter plan and up).

Is it free?

The Free plan covers 1 company, 1 site and up to 10 active employees, with no credit card and no end date. Paid plans start at $19.49 per month in Canadian dollars.
