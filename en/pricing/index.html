Questions

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free plan needs no credit card.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, from your account at any time. Your data stays where it is.

Is the Scheduling add-on included?

No. It is $14.95/month CAD on any paid plan, and you can try it free for 14 days.

Can I get a refund?

No. Payments are not refundable, including partial months, so try the paid features free for 14 days first. If you cancel, you keep your plan until the end of the month already paid and are not charged again.
